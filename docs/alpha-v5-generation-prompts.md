## Общая часть для всех шести листов

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite animation sheet for a side-view mobile fighting game. Create one clean square 2×2 sheet with exactly four sequential full-body frames, read left-to-right and then top-to-bottom. Use the supplied RUMIR Alpha sheet as a strict identity and costume reference: the same adult male wolf-human fighter, silver hair, wolf ears, grey tail, black martial outfit with red straps, red-black armored boots and cyan energy around both gloves. Preserve face, costume, proportions, camera angle, character scale and floor baseline. Side view facing right, one complete fighter in each quadrant, generous padding, no overlap. Perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background with no shadow, gradient, texture, reflection or floor plane. No text, captions, dividers, border, watermark or extra objects. Avoid duplicate limbs, cropped ears, fists, boots or tail, costume changes, weapons and random poses.

## WALK

1. Guard stance, weight begins shifting forward.
2. Front foot steps right, rear heel lifts.
3. Hips pass over the planted front foot, rear leg passes through.
4. Rear foot lands and guard stabilizes for a seamless loop.

## JUMP

1. Compact takeoff crouch with knees bent and guard raised.
2. Rising jump, both feet off the ground and knees drawing upward.
3. Apex in balanced airborne guard, tail counterbalancing.
4. Controlled landing with knees absorbing impact.

## CROUCH

1. Normal guard begins lowering.
2. Deep stable combat crouch, forearms protecting head and ribs.
3. Held low guard with centered weight and one glove slightly extended.
4. Rise back toward normal guard.

## DODGE

1. Guard slips the head and upper body away from an incoming straight.
2. Rearward step left, front shoulder pulled away, hands protect the jaw.
3. Maximum balanced evasive lean and distance.
4. Recovery step to stable guard facing right.

## KNOCKDOWN

1. Severe non-graphic sports stagger backward.
2. Side/back fall with one hand reaching to break the fall.
3. Down on one side/back, knees bent and forearm braced.
4. Recovery begins on one knee, preparing to stand.

## RUMIR DISCHARGE

1. Wide grounded guard, cyan electricity concentrates between both hands.
2. Deep stance, hands compress a circular cyan core with wolf-head contours.
3. Forward release, a compact cyan spectral wolf-head blast originates from the hands.
4. Follow-through with extended arms and fading cyan arcs.
